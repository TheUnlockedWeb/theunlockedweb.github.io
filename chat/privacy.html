Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

TUW Community Chat ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle user data.

1. Information We Collect

When you sign in using OAuth providers (Google, Microsoft, GitHub) or Firebase Auth, we receive basic profile information such as your display name and email address necessary for chat functionality.

2. How We Use Information

Your information is used strictly to authenticate your identity within the TUW Community Chat application and display your messages.

3. Data Security

We rely on secure protocols provided by Firebase Auth and industry standards to protect your credentials.
